# AI Infrastructure Intelligence Brief — prepared for Crusoe

*November 7, 2026*

## Top 3 developments

**1.**
- **What happened:** Reuters reports that Google is acquiring a Wyoming data center site from Crusoe. Only the headline is available, so terms and rationale are not stated.
- **Why it matters to Crusoe:** The sale takes a Wyoming site out of Crusoe's own development pipeline, which the profile puts at more than 40 GW. Depending on terms, it could either recycle capital toward the Texas and Missouri campuses or show that the site was hard to develop. The thing to watch is whether Crusoe or Google discloses the price, capacity and any continuing Crusoe role at the site.
- **Business impact:**
  - **Direction:** Risk
  - **Channel:** Customers & pipeline
  - **Size:** unsized
  - **Consider:** Whether any proceeds or teams freed up by the Wyoming sale go to the two other Texas campuses or the Missouri campus, and whether Crusoe keeps a build or supply role with Google there.
- **Confidence:** Reported
- **Source:** [Google acquires Wyoming data center site from AI infrastructure firm Crusoe](https://www.reuters.com/business/google-acquires-wyoming-data-center-site-ai-infrastructure-firm-crusoe-2026-11-06/) (Reuters)
- <sub>**Evidence:** "Google acquires Wyoming data center site from AI infrastructure firm Crusoe" (RSS summary)</sub>

**2.**
- **What happened:** CoreWeave's 2032 bond yield has risen above 16%, and default protection costs have climbed across AI-linked companies, including Oracle. Goldman Sachs says moratoriums could affect 45% of planned US capacity, much of it in Texas.
- **Why it matters to Crusoe:** Higher credit spreads for neoclouds and for Oracle could raise borrowing costs for Crusoe Cloud and for the Abilene, TX campuses, and could weigh on the valuation of Crusoe's reported IPO. A Texas moratorium could also slow Crusoe's Texas builds. Watch whether Texas governor-elect Gina Hinojosa's proposed moratorium advances, and whether CoreWeave's CDS widens past its 1,289 bp peak.
- **Business impact:**
  - **Direction:** Risk
  - **Channel:** Financing & cost of capital
  - **Size:** 1,289 basis points (stated in the source)
  - **Consider:** Whether to lock in project-level financing for the Abilene campuses now, given Goldman's forecast of $300 billion in project-level issuance competing for investors next year.
- **Confidence:** Reported
- **Source:** [The AI ‘credit card’ is getting costlier as CoreWeave bond yield tops 16%](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/11/06/coreweave-bond-yield-tops-16percent-as-ai-credit-stress-rises.html) (CNBC)
- <sub>**Evidence:** "The yield on its 2032 bond with a 9.625% coupon has climbed above 16%." (article text) · **Size:** "Coreweave's five-year credit default swaps spiked above 1,289 basis points on Thursday" (article text)</sub>

**3.**
- **What happened:** Citing Bloomberg, a low-tier outlet reports that ING is selling a portfolio of about $500 million in data center loans, including Stargate-linked financing, with bids due November 19. It also reports that Oracle issued a force majeure notice to developers on Stargate New Mexico.
- **Why it matters to Crusoe:** Oracle is the customer for Crusoe's 1.2 GW Abilene campus, so a reported Oracle move to protect its exit options on another Stargate site raises counterparty risk for that relationship and could make Oracle-backed project debt harder to price. The test is the price ING's loans clear at after the November 19 bid deadline, and whether Oracle's notice extends beyond New Mexico.
- **Business impact:**
  - **Direction:** Risk
  - **Channel:** Customers & pipeline
  - **Size:** $500 million (stated in the source)
  - **Consider:** Whether Crusoe's Abilene contract with Oracle has force majeure terms similar to those reportedly invoked in New Mexico, and how lenders to the Abilene campus view that exposure.
- **Confidence:** Reported
- **Source:** [ING Discounts $500 Million Data Center Loan Portfolio in Fire Sale, Signaling Exit from Stargate Project Financing](https://finance.biggo.com/news/4713f8b3-a8ab-423a-883a-c378324e61a6) (BigGo Finance)
- <sub>**Evidence:** "Oracle has issued a force majeure notice to developers on the Stargate New Mexico project." (article text) · **Size:** "ING is seeking to sell a portfolio of about $500 million in data center loans in a single transaction" (article text)</sub>
