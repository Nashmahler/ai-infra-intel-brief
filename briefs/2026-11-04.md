# Crusoe Intelligence Brief — November 4, 2026

## Top 3 developments

**1.**
- **What happened:** Bloomberg reports that CoreWeave bonds hit new lows as Texas data centers come into political crosshairs.
- **Why it matters to Crusoe:** Crusoe's Abilene campuses for Oracle and Microsoft and its other Texas campuses sit in the same state. Political pressure on Texas data centers could affect their permitting, operating terms or financing costs, and the bond selloff suggests lenders may already be repricing that risk. The open questions are whether Texas officials take concrete action against data centers and whether CoreWeave's debt keeps sliding.

**2.**
- **What happened:** The Washington Examiner reports that anti-data center ballot measures are on track to pass 40-1, which it describes as a sign of backlash.
- **Why it matters to Crusoe:** Crusoe's pipeline of more than 40 GW, plus new sites beyond its Texas and Missouri campuses, depends on local approval. Ballot measures restricting data centers could narrow where those projects can be sited or slow their approval. The next markers are the certified results and whether any passed measures cover jurisdictions where Crusoe has sites or plans them.

**3.**
- **What happened:** Data Center Dynamics reports that Nvidia has told data center partners to cut their China exposure and is limiting RTX chip sales to mainland China, as the US and China look to decouple.
- **Why it matters to Crusoe:** Crusoe Cloud runs on Nvidia GPUs. Partner requirements like these could add compliance conditions to Crusoe's GPU supply relationship, though the story does not name Crusoe, so the link is indirect. That would change if Nvidia formalizes the requirements in partner terms, and whether it does so is the thing to watch.
- **Confidence:** Reported
- **Source:** [Nvidia tells data center partners to cut China exposure, limits RTX chip sales to mainland](https://www.datacenterdynamics.com/en/news/nvidia-tells-data-center-partners-to-cut-china-exposure-limits-rtx-chip-sales-to-mainland/) (Data Center Dynamics)
