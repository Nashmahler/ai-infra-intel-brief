# crusoe-intel-brief
Daily, source-verified intelligence brief on AI infrastructure — compute, power, data centers, and chips. Collects news, ranks what matters, writes a concise brief, and verifies every claim against its source before publishing.

## What's next

- **SEC filings.** EDGAR feeds for CoreWeave, Nebius, and Oracle were cut from v1. The SEC rejects requests without a descriptive User-Agent that includes contact info, so adding them means setting a `SEC_USER_AGENT` secret (e.g. `crusoe-intel-brief you@example.com`) and reading it at fetch time.
- Primary-source monitoring beyond filings: regulator dockets and interconnection queues.
- A structured market map of companies, customers, and capacity.
- Weekly trend summaries.
- Email delivery.
